# Business Brief

## Situation
A telecom business loses customers every month. Each lost customer reduces recurring revenue and future value. The retention team needs to know where churn sits, which customers are at highest risk, and which actions to prioritise.

## Stakeholders and decisions

| Stakeholder | Decision | What they need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retention manager | Which customers to target first | High-risk segments and their churn rates |
| Product manager | Whether to change contract or service offers | Churn by contract and service |
| Customer success lead | How to support new customers | Churn and tenure, especially early tenure |
| Finance partner | Size of the revenue at risk | Charges and churn by segment |

## Business questions

| # | Question | Decision it supports |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | What share of customers churn? | Baseline for every retention target |
| Q2 | Which contract, payment and service types go with churn? | Offer and contract changes |
| Q3 | Which demographic and household groups go with churn? | Targeting |
| Q4 | How do tenure and monthly charges differ between churned and retained customers? | Onboarding and pricing review |
| Q5 | Which combinations of factors identify the highest-risk customers? | Proactive retention campaigns |

## Scope
- **In:** the 21 fields of the customer dataset, as described in `02_data_and_quality.md`.
- **Out:** causes of churn, competitor offers, customer satisfaction, and revenue forecasts.

## Constraints
- A single snapshot. No time series.
- Secondary public dataset, not checked against company records.
